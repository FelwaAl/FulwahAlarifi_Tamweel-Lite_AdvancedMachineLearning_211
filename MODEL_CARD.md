# بطاقة النموذج | Model Card
## الحالة
READY_FOR_REVIEW — جودة التفسير تحتاج مراجعة بشرية، وليست درجة آلية.
## الغرض والاستخدام | Purpose and use
Educational use only, on synthetic Tamweel Lite data, to practise validation, threshold policy and calibration. A decision of 1 is a simulated review flag; 0 is not an approval or a safety guarantee. It must not be used for real financing approval or refusal, for decisions about real people or regions, or as evidence of fairness.
بيانات Tamweel Lite اصطناعية؛ الهدف حدث خلال 90 يومًا بعد الطلب. 22 خاصية متاحة وقت الطلب؛ لا معرفات أو تواريخ أو هدف في المدخلات. الاستخدام التعليمي فقط؛ لا قرارات تمويل فعلية.
## البيانات والتحقق | Data and validation
حوض التدريب والاختيار: 6576 طلبًا. المعايرة: 836 طلبًا و78 موجبًا. حجز عملاء المعايرة، 90 يومًا لنضج التسميات، وOOF أمامي متداخل مع فصل العملاء في المستويين. طيات المقارنة: 2023Q1 و2023Q3 و2024Q1. نستبعد النتائج التي لم تنضج قبل الأدوار التالية؛ آخر بيانات التدريب لا تستخدم تلقائيًا.
Candidates were compared with nested forward OOF on 2,155 rows across folds 2023Q1, 2023Q3 and 2024Q1: training always precedes validation with time for the 90-day outcome to mature, customers are separated, and ensemble weights and the stacker were learned on inner OOF only. Limits: the three folds share overlapping training histories, so fold SD is descriptive, not a confidence interval or significance test. The threshold was chosen on the same OOF labels, so its loss (1,111 units) is optimistic. The training data was used earlier in the course, so this is not a final untouched test, and challenge labels are unavailable.
## النموذج والقرار | Model selection
KEEP SINGLE / Logistic. انحراف AP المرجعي: 0.029808. ارجع إلى artifacts/ensemble_comparison.csv وday5_fold_scores.csv للأرقام الكاملة.
KEEP SINGLE: Logistic Regression. It had the best mean AP across three forward folds (0.392, fold SD 0.030) and the best Brier (0.0633). No ensemble beat it: Weighted 0.389 (-0.002), Stack 0.383 (-0.009), Equal 0.372 (-0.020), so none cleared the gate of gaining more than one fold SD. The base models were highly correlated (0.88-0.96), so combining them had little to add. Stack also had the worst ECE (0.031) because it rescales probabilities. Weighted's 0.002 gap is noise, and three models cost more to run, monitor and explain, so the extra complexity did not earn its place.
## المعايرة | Calibration
Sigmoid على عينة محجوزة من التدريب والاختيار؛ الرسم والمقاييس تشخيص على عينة تعلم المعاير، وليسا اختبارًا مستقلاً. لا ادعاء بتحسن على تحدٍّ مجهول التسميات.
The sigmoid was learned on a reserved calibration period (836 rows, 78 defaults) held out from training and selection. It did not improve calibration: Brier rose from 0.0765 to 0.0781, log-loss from 0.266 to 0.277 and ECE from 0.021 to 0.035, while AP and AUC were unchanged. The unweighted Logistic was likely already well calibrated, leaving little to correct. These are fit diagnostics on the same rows used to learn the sigmoid, not an independent evaluation, and no calibration claim can be made on the unlabeled challenge data.
## السياسة والسعة والمناطق | Policy and regions
خسارة 10 FN + FP، عتبة OOF الخام 0.16892161427109176 والمنقولة 0.12225843144286948. سعة الدفعة 300؛ المرشحون 330؛ الإشارات النهائية 300. كتلة الدرجات المتساوية لا تقسم. 1=إشارة مراجعة تعليمية، 0=عدم رفع الإشارة.
On OOF, the threshold 0.169 flagged 245 of 2,155 requests (11.4%; busiest period 11.7%), catching 84 of 179 defaulters (recall 46.9%, precision 34.3%). On the 2,500-request challenge batch, 330 requests (13.2%) exceeded the transported threshold, so the 12% cap kept the top 300 and removed 30, keeping tied scores together. Regional OOF FPR ranged from 6.3% (eastern) to 10.3% (western), about a 4-point gap, and western also had the lowest recall (44.7%), the same pattern as Day 3. With about 490 negatives and only 35-49 defaulters per region, this is descriptive and noisy, not a fairness certificate, but the repeated western pattern needs review.
## التفسير وحدوده | Explanation scope
تفسير اليوم الرابع يخص نموذج اليوم الرابع؛ لا يُنسب تلقائيًا إلى هذه النسخة. تغيير النموذج أو خصائصه أو معايرته يستلزم مراجعة التفسير.
Day 4 explanations belong to the Day 4 weighted LightGBM. The final model is an unweighted Logistic Regression with a different calibration, so those SHAP values and reason codes cannot be attributed to it. For the final version I would need to recheck feature contributions (coefficients or SHAP on this model), regenerate reason statements, repeat the local stability check and confirm whether bureau_score and dti still dominate.
## المتابعة والقيود | Monitoring and limitations
Track each batch's flag volume against the 12% cap, since the challenge batch already exceeded it before capping (13.2%). Monitor score distribution and prevalence for drift. Once 90-day outcomes mature, recheck AP, Brier and ECE and whether the sigmoid still helps or should be removed through a governed update. Recheck regional FPR and recall with their denominators, especially western. Any threshold or model change must be developed and validated on new data, not tuned on the batch being judged.
OOF يستخدم للاختيار، وثلاث فترات ليست اختبار دلالة. العتبة قد تتغير سعتها عند نقلها إلى نموذج معاد التدريب. لا تسميات للتحدي، ولا مقاييس أداء أو شهادة عدالة له. البيانات لا تمثل أشخاصًا أو مناطق حقيقية.
